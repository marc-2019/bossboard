**Parties (fill per deal):**

- **Controller:** Customer organisation (name, NZBN/company number, address)  
- **Processor:** Instilligent Limited (NZBN 9429041896853), Auckland, New Zealand  

## 1. Subject matter

Processor provides the BossBoard software service (hosting, support, optional AI-assisted document drafting features) so Controller’s authorised users can manage trade-business operations data.

## 2. Duration

From subscription start until termination + any retention period stated in the Privacy Policy or SOW (default: account deletion request fulfilled within commercially reasonable time after verification).

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

- Account and authentication data  
- Business content entered by users (e.g. invoices, quotes, SWMS drafts, job notes, photos, team membership)  
- Technical logs required for security and reliability  
- Optional AI model calls for SWMS/suggestion features (content may be sent to configured AI providers under Processor’s subprocessors list)

Purpose: deliver, secure, and support the Service; improve reliability; meet legal obligations.

## 4. Types of personal information

May include names, emails, phone numbers, business identifiers, job/site details, photos, IP/device metadata. Controller determines categories of data subjects (staff, customers of Controller, subcontractors).

## 5. Obligations of Processor

- Process only on documented instructions of Controller (including this DPA and product configuration).  
- Ensure confidentiality of personnel with access.  
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (access control, encryption in transit, backups, logging).  
- Not sell personal information.  
- Assist Controller with reasonable Privacy Act / applicable rights requests (access, correction, deletion) relating to Service data.  
- Notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller’s data.  
- Delete or return personal data on termination per instructions, except where retention is required by law.  
- Make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance; allow audits under agreed process (notice, scope, confidentiality).

## 6. Obligations of Controller

- Ensure lawful basis and notices for data it enters.  
- Not upload special-category or prohibited data unless agreed in writing.  
- Configure users and roles appropriately.  
- Remain responsible for final SWMS/compliance content (AI drafts are assistive only).

## 7. Subprocessors

Processor may use infrastructure and AI subprocessors (e.g. hosting, email, payment, AI inference). Current high-level list is maintained in the Privacy Policy / customer appendix. Material changes: notify Controller where required by agreement.

## 8. International transfers

Processing may occur in regions where subprocessors operate. Processor takes reasonable steps consistent with NZ Privacy Act expectations for cross-border disclosure.

## 9. Liability and order of precedence

Commercial liability caps follow the main Terms / Order Form. If conflict: Order Form → this DPA → Terms → Privacy Policy for processing topics.

## 10. Governing law

New Zealand law. Courts of New Zealand (non-exclusive if parties agree otherwise in the Order Form).
