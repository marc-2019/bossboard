Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

1. Who We Are

BossBoard is operated by Instilligent Limited, a New Zealand registered company. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand) and the Information Privacy Principles.

Contact: privacy@instilligent.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, trade type, business name, and business address when you register.
  • Business Data: Invoices, quotes, expenses, job logs, SWMS documents, certifications, and team member information you create within the app.
  • Photos: Receipt photos, site photos, and other images you upload to the app.
  • Payment Information: If you subscribe in the iOS app, Apple processes the purchase. We do not receive your card number from Apple. Website payments, if used, are processed by Stripe. We do not store card details ourselves.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and push notification tokens for delivering app notifications.
  • Location (approximate and/or precise): When you grant permission while using the app, for job-site / SWMS address assist — not continuous background advertising tracking.
  • Usage Data: Feature usage patterns to help us improve the app. This data is anonymised and aggregated.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the BossBoard service
  • Generate compliance documents (SWMS) using AI assistance
  • Send invoices and quotes to your customers on your behalf
  • Send push notifications for certification expiry reminders
  • Process iOS subscriptions through the App Store, and website payments through Stripe if used
  • Communicate with you about your account, updates, and support
  • Improve our service through anonymised usage analytics

4. AI-Powered Features (third-party AI — limited use)

BossBoard uses Anthropic Claude for optional SWMS hazard/control suggestions. Processing is limited use for the document you requested — not for ads or selling data.

  • When the in-app AI-Powered Generation toggle is on, trade type, job description, and site details you enter are sent via our API to Anthropic
  • Toggle off = template hazards only; no third-party AI call for that request (that toggle is your consent control)
  • We do not send passwords or card numbers to the AI service
  • Anthropic processes under commercial API terms; you must review all generated content before site use

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Apple: For iOS App Store subscriptions (Apple ID and purchase receipt; we do not receive your card number)
  • Stripe: For website payments only (not iOS in-app purchases)
  • Anthropic: For AI document generation (job descriptions and trade type only)
  • Your Customers: When you send invoices or share documents via the client portal
  • Your Team Members: When you invite team members, they can see shared business data as permitted by their role
  • Email Service Provider: For sending transactional emails (invoice delivery, verification codes)

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2/1.3) and at rest
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token handling
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular database backups
  • Role-based access controls for team features

7. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a standard format
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@instilligent.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Personal account data is deleted within 30 days
  • Business documents (invoices, SWMS) may be retained for up to 7 years as required by New Zealand tax and compliance obligations
  • Anonymised usage data may be retained indefinitely for service improvement

9. Cookies

The BossBoard mobile app does not use cookies. Our website uses only essential cookies for basic functionality and does not use third-party tracking cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes via email or in-app notification. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@instilligent.com
  • Company: Instilligent Limited, New Zealand

12. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy concern, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
